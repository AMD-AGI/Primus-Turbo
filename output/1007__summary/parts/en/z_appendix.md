### A.3 FLOP conventions

- **Current convention** (op-evolve `tools/op_flops.py`): per call, fwd 2.199292e12 and bwd 5.498229e12 (5-GEMM nominal count, causal taken as (s+1)/(2s)), fwd+bwd 7.697e12; ms = FLOP ÷ TF/s. Values marked `*` in the (b) tables and labeled derived in the (c) tables are all converted this way.
- **Documents from 09-13..09-16**: report only the fwd+bwd total (7.697e12 ÷ total time). The per-direction TF/s in the (a)(b) tables were converted from the single-direction times.
- **7-GEMM issued convention** (not used in this report): the in-tree vendored fused bwd issues 7 GEMM passes, issued 7.6975e12, 1.400× the nominal; our FlyDSL bwd (r0 to s6) issues 7.7395e12, 1.40764× the nominal (including the diagonal-block granularity ×1.00546); aiter ASM issues only 1.0155× the nominal. So s6's "103.8% of ASM" is achieved while doing ~39% more matrix math. The 0913/0914 documents at one point reported bwd on the issued convention (e.g. B0 vendored fused bwd 748.4 vs 534.6 TF/s nominal).
- **Other old conventions**: 09-23 STAGE2-FWD-SWEEP used fwd ≈2.233e12 (old harness, TF/s ~1.5% too high); the 5.8 TF/s in 09-17 DKDV-FIRST-TIMING is a single-head custom "useful" convention and not comparable; the 09-14 B0 Triton op-evolve job counts bwd only.
- **GEMM**: TF/s = 2MNK ÷ time.
- **e2e**: tokens/s is single-card throughput; TFLOP/s is torchtitan's self-reported value, or is converted from tps on torchtitan's convention (32 layers ~57.9 GFLOP/token, 8 layers ~16.84); torchtitan's MFU uses the A100's 312 TF/s as its denominator and is entirely unusable.
- **Ratio direction**: "relative to ASM" in (b) = this row's time ÷ ASM time (>1 means slower than ASM); "% of ASM" in (c) = ASM time ÷ this row's time (>100% means faster than ASM). The two are reciprocals of each other; take care when quoting them.

### A.5 Noise floor

- **op**: the same-session noise floor of this op is 0.24–0.66% (before 09-24 the 1.57% of the HipKittens GEMM ladder was mistakenly used); fwd cross-session drift ~1.5%; the same r7 code read 382.73 → 404.06 TF/s (+5.6%) before vs after an AC power cycle; blocked A/A is fwd ±0.19%, bwd ±0.05%; the acceptance min_gain is 0.007 (0 for bwd early on).
- **fast shape**: ~55 µs per call, dominated by launch overhead. Two arms running the same kernel differ by only 0.05% in min, but can differ by 56% in median, so fast was later switched to scoring by min, with its weight set to 0.
- **e2e**: on A0-VR, before nkfix the results were trimodal, with cross-run sd 3.91% (between clusters 1.8% / 6.8%); after nkfix sd 0.42%; on B0 one process stepped down as a whole by ~5% midway (2,015 → 1,918 tps, cause not investigated); cutting the step count from 20 to 10 changed the median of steps 4–8 by ≤0.22%.
- **Card contention**: on B0 09-14, with all four cards fully loaded, per-card timing drifted by up to 50%; sustained GEMMs on a neighboring card make attention on the card under test 3–11× slower.

### A.6 Inconsistencies between sections: reconciled and unresolved

The first version listed here 11 records from the four sections that contradicted each other. For the ones that could be verified, this version corrected the main text and lists them under "Reconciled" (if an old statement still lingers somewhere, this section prevails); the rest are either not yet verified or are differences in convention that do not amount to contradictions, and are listed under "Unresolved or different conventions".

**Reconciled**

- **torchtitan version of the A0 09-13 e2e**: v0.2.2 (Primus `third_party/torchtitan` pinned at `73a0e6979`). The "0.1.0" written in `0914__campaign/RESULTS.md:65` is a typo.
- **"Measured VR throttle cost ~1.93×"**: 1.88–2.01× (median 1.93×) is the A0-VR→B0 cross-card ratio (B0 itself also has a ~9% VR cost), not A0's own throttle cost. A0's own before/after-reflash factors are op 1.24–1.67× and e2e 1.44× (the latter with additional differences). The 09-13 1.65× is a clock-ratio estimate and is no longer called void (see A.2).
- **"ASM attention differs across machines by only 1.12–1.15×"**: only the fwd 1.118× (1.572 vs 1.406 ms) holds; the bwd item compares the shim artifact 10.160 ms with fused Triton 8.835 ms and does not hold (see A.2, d.3).
- **The 220.6 TF/s in the `c1325c7e` commit message**: measurement conditions unknown (the commit message states neither hkv, nor whether causal, nor the clock); which clock ratio 220.6 / 129.4 = 1.70× matches is undetermined. This report no longer says "it was measured on an unthrottled card".
- **AC count**: d.1.4 is authoritative. A0 had 27 manual power cycles/reboots because of wedges, 28 counting the ineffective AC on 09-29; of these, five were on 09-15 and nine on 09-16 (UTC).
- **FlyDSL fwd speedup after reflash**: 1.47–1.50×; order o1 2.023→1.353 ms is 1.50×, order o2 1.992→1.356 ms is 1.47×, mean ~1.48× (`0928__a0_repro/REPORT.md` §6.1).

**Unresolved or different conventions**

1. **Source of the 09-14 B0 "turbo vs flex 1.459×"**: the retraction note in (b) b.3 says "the difference comes from other turbo patches (float8 / mx linear)"; (a) a.3 #2 and #11 consider this attribution doubtful (going by the Primus code on this machine, it is not apparent how these two patches would take effect under `converters: []`); all that is certain is that it does not come from attention. Unresolved.
2. **aiter Triton MHA factory-default readings (09-13 34.565 ms vs 09-15 28.035 ms)**: (b) b.2.1 cites the 0915 assessment that the 09-13 row is off by +23% and unreliable; (a) a.2 #12 considers the 09-15 run the more likely anomaly (its fwd 3.725 ms looks like a tuned forward). Not re-measured; unresolved.
3. **B0 clock during training**: (b) E4 gives 1.25–1.45 GHz, (c) B0-28 gives 1.34–1.39 GHz, (d) gives 1.25–1.69 GHz. The former lean toward the whole-step sclk mean, the latter is a reading of the attention window during training; the conventions differ, and they have not been cross-checked item by item.
4. **A0-VR loaded clock**: the sections give 0.94 / 0.95 / 0.97–1.07 GHz respectively, plus a single-point sample of 943 MHz. All refer to the loaded clock under the VR throttle; the differences come from different measurement windows.
5. **Real gain of L21**: REPORT-0928.html says +0.3%, the blocked reading in ruler/REPORT.md is +0.6% (already listed at the end of (d)). Unresolved.
6. **10-02 e2e fwd/bwd split (wording reconciled, one item still not broken down)**: "bwd faster by 4.5 ms per step" in the main text of `1002__e2e/RESULT-e2e.md` is the median of adjacent paired differences (−4.50 / −4.56 ms in the two processes); subtracting the two arms' own medians gives 3.8–4.4 ms (166.54−162.15, 166.41−162.62; 3.9–4.3 after rounding in the RESULT-e2e.md table). The two do not contradict each other, and this report uniformly writes "~4 ms". What is still not broken down: fly's attention total per step is ~4.3–5.2 ms more than ASM's, yet the step is 0.7–2.0 ms faster; step − FA is −6.3 ms in both processes, beyond the 5 ms threshold of criterion 2 in E2E-PLAN §4; the same holds for s6 vs r29 (attention −53.0 ms, while the step is only 37.0–39.1 ms faster). This difference lies outside attention or in timing noise [`1002__e2e/e2e/runs/analysis.1002_095504.txt`].
